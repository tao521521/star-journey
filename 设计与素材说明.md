# 设计与素材说明

项目名：星火征程。主张：把长征精神，写进青春。

视觉采用朱红 #b92c24、暖金 #ba945d、纸张白 #faf7f1 与深棕 #342c26。电脑侧栏与手机底栏共享六个内容入口，突出学习路径和个人成长，不使用虚构用户数据或排行榜。

主插画保存位置：`dist/assets/long-march-woodcut.png`。使用内置 image_gen 工具，2026年11月10日生成。作为艺术表达使用。未提供任何《前进之路》截图作为输入或编辑目标。

完整生成提示词：

> Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: original hero illustration for a Chinese educational interactive game commemorating the Long March, 90th anniversary. Create an exquisite widescreen 3:2 landscape original Chinese woodcut and layered paper-cut illustration. A small anonymous group of Chinese Red Army soldiers in historically plausible 1930s cloth uniforms and caps, seen from behind, traveling uphill on a winding ridge with one large flowing plain crimson flag (no emblem), monumental overlapping mountains, a distant narrow suspension bridge, river flowing through valley, glorious sunrise circle. Crisp sophisticated hand-carved print textures, expressive rock strata and clouds, dramatic composition with travelers in lower right, mountain peaks in upper right, quieter open atmosphere to left. Palette warm parchment ivory, cinnabar vermilion, deep oxblood red and muted antique gold, cohesive warm powerful hopeful atmosphere, premium museum editorial illustration, detailed but restrained, dignified and inspiring. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no contemporary buildings, no weapons close-up, no resemblance to existing game art. Art illustration only, not a website screenshot.

字体调用操作系统已有中文字体，不打包分发字体文件。图标以本项目SVG路径绘制。历史卡片中的大字、纹理、示意路线由CSS与SVG绘制。

史料转述与教学模拟在页面中明确区分；人物介绍采用非肖像文字卡片，没有使用未经许可的人物照片。来源链接统一收录在 `dist/data.js`，网页中可逐项访问。
